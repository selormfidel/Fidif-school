— Our History

Over a Decade of Excellence

Fidif School Complex is an excellent academic institution established by Mr. Daniel Dziko in the year 2011 to provide pre-school, Primary and Junior High School (JHS) education to the nation Ghana and the world at large.

Our journey has been one of steady growth — building modern facilities, raising academic standards, and earning the trust of families across the community.

Our Mission

“To provide quality education, ICT training, instill Christian values, inspire the child for academic excellence and ensure staff development.”

Our Vision

“To be the best and most prestigious academic institution in Ga-East.”
